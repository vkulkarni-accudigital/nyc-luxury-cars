The Service

Precision
Transport.

Moving a luxury vehicle is not a logistics problem. It is a trust exercise. Our white-glove delivery service treats every vehicle as if it were irreplaceable, because to our clients, it often is.

We operate an enclosed flatbed fleet servicing the entire NYC metro area. Every driver is trained in exotic vehicle handling protocols. Every delivery is documented with pre- and post-transport photography, so you know exactly what we received and exactly what we returned.

How It Works

From Call
to Keys.

01

Book and Confirm

Tell us the pickup address, delivery address, and your preferred time window. We confirm availability and assign a driver within the hour.

02

Pickup and Load

Our driver arrives with an enclosed transporter, photographs the vehicle comprehensively, and loads it using soft-tie systems with zero chassis contact.

03

Deliver and Confirm

We deliver to your specified location, unload with the same care, walk you through the condition report, and hand over the keys. Done.
